## Overview

A deep aubergine on white — a purple dark enough that it reads as ink until you
put it next to black and see the hue. It appears only where the product wants a
decision, and nowhere else.

Reach for this when the interface is a workspace people live in and the brand
needs to be present without ever being loud.

## Colors

White canvas with pure `#000000` body copy at 21:1, and a single muted tier at
`#696969`, 5.49:1. Two text values, no more.

The accent is `#611f69`, measuring **11:1** against white — extraordinarily high
for an accent, and the reason it can carry white text at the same ratio. Most
brand colours sit near the 4.5:1 floor; this one has headroom to spare, which is
what lets it be used for text as well as for fills.

Surfaces are `#f9f0ff`, a lilac so pale it is almost white — the aubergine
diluted rather than a neutral grey. Borders are `#f5f5f5` at 1.09:1, separation
only.

## Typography

Salesforce-Sans at 18px for body, with line height left at `normal`, and
Salesforce-Avant-Garde at weight 700 for headings. Two families doing clearly
separate jobs.

The extractor found 21 distinct sizes and collapsed them to twelve, which is a
sign the page is assembled from more components than it has a scale for.

## Layout

**No spacing grid**, though it comes close: the best candidate explains 74.6% of
observed values, just under the threshold. The run is 2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12, 16, 20,
24, 32 — which is a 4px grid with enough exceptions to break it.

**1230px measure, 80px between sections, eleven sections.** The nav is 80px and
**absolutely positioned**, so it sits over the hero rather than above it, which
is why the hero's colour runs to the very top of the page.

The hero is **0.87 viewports, full bleed**, with a 58px left-aligned headline,
one media element and two filled calls to action.

**Section composition across eleven sections: full-bleed, text-led, moderate
copy.** All eleven bleed, five carry a repeating group, but **only one is
image-led** — unusual for a page that feels this illustrated, because the
artwork is largely inline SVG below the size threshold rather than photography.
The median section holds **320 characters**.

## Elevation & Depth

Wide and soft: `rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.1) 0px 0px 32px` is an unoffset 32px blur, a
glow rather than a drop shadow. Things lift straight up here, they do not lean.

## Shapes

Radii of 4, 8, 16 and 48px, with buttons at the smallest. The 48px step is for
large containers, not controls.

## Components

Buttons are padded `12px` all round; links carry a much larger `19px 40px 20px
40px`, which is navigation-sized rather than inline. Every state — hover, focus,
focus-visible, active — is declared on both.

**Motion here is slow and expressive: 0.42s cubic-bezier(0.165, 0.84, 0.44, 1)**
— four times Linear's duration, on an aggressive ease-out. It is the longest
transition measured in this registry, and it is deliberate.

## Do's and Don'ts

- **Do** keep the aubergine dark. Its 11:1 contrast is what makes it usable for
  text, and lightening it toward a conventional purple costs that.
- **Do** tint surfaces rather than greying them.
- **Do** let the motion be slow. 0.42s on an aggressive ease-out is four times
  Linear's, and it is the most distinctive behavioural fact in this file.
- **Don't** add a third text tier. Two values and a size scale carry hierarchy.
- **Don't** rely on the `#f5f5f5` border for structure; at 1.09:1 it is barely
  there.
- **Don't** lean on photography. Only one of eleven sections is image-led.

**What this file does not constrain:** page structure, section order, or
content. It gives you a colour discipline, a type pairing and a tempo.
