## Overview

Near-black controls on an almost-white canvas, Inter under Manrope, a strict 4px
grid, and a full dark palette alongside. The brand green appears as a status
colour rather than as the accent.

Reach for this when the product is developer infrastructure and the interface
should read as precise rather than expressive.

## Colors

`#fdfdfd` — very slightly off-white — under `#030303` at **20.3:1**, with one
muted tier at `#696969`, 5.42:1. Surfaces are `#f6f5fd`, faintly violet rather
than grey.

The accent is **`#0c0f24`, a near-black navy**, taken from filled buttons and
carrying `#fefefe` labels. The green everyone associates with this brand is
`#3fcf8e`, and the extractor found it as **`success`** — a status colour — not
as the primary. That is what the page actually does with it.

A **dark palette** was observed and is carried in the `dark-` tokens.

## Typography

**Inter at 15px**/1.5 at weight **450** — a variable-font weight between regular
and medium, which is not a value most systems can express. Headings are
**Manrope** at 600.

The scale is long: 12, 13, 14, 15, 16, 18, 22, 25.6, 32, 34, 46.

## Layout

**A 4px base unit** explaining 88% of observed spacing.

**Section composition: full-bleed, text-led, dense copy**, a median of 660
characters a section.

**Two figures here are weak and should be checked.** The measure came out at
560px and the hero headline at 16px, neither of which matches a page of this
kind. Only 2083 of 23229 elements were sampled — this is a heavy application
shell — so the layout numbers rest on a thin slice. The colour and type tokens
are drawn from far more evidence and are sound.

## Elevation & Depth

The shadow values are **ring stacks**: several fully transparent layers with one
live layer among them, which is how utility frameworks express a focus ring
without changing box size. One reads
`oklab(0.999994 ... / 0.3) 0px 0px 0px 1px`; another is a plain
`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12) 0 0 0 1px inset`.

The canvas carries a linear wash from `#030303` at 8% to 4% over 63% of painted
background area, with a `backdrop-filter: blur(4px)`, a linear mask and a
`drop-shadow`.

## Shapes

Radii of 4, 6, 8, 12 and 16px with buttons at 6px, plus a fully-rounded pill
value for circular controls.

## Components

Buttons take `16px 0px` padding; links `40px 24px` at an 8px radius with a 1px
border. Both were measured from few elements out of many distinct shapes, so
treat the box metrics as indicative.

**Motion is 0.15s** on `cubic-bezier(0.4, 0, 0.2, 1)`.

State rules were found but **none of them target elements on this page** — the
site's own CSS is likely among the four cross-origin stylesheets that could not
be read.

## Do's and Don'ts

- **Do** treat `#3fcf8e` as a status colour. The page fills its buttons with
  near-black, not with green.
- **Do** keep the 450 body weight if the typeface supports it; it is between the
  usual steps on purpose.
- **Do** hold the 4px grid.
- **Don't** trust the 560px measure or the 16px hero headline from this capture.
  Both come from a thin sample of a large application shell.
- **Don't** read the empty state list as "no states"; most of this site's CSS
  was not readable.

**What this file does not constrain:** page structure, which this capture
measured poorly, and states, which it could not see.

### The base

*Shared by every file in this registry; only the counts below are this
file's.*

**Budget.** 10 colours, 7 more for its dark scheme, 6 radii, 10 spacing steps,
11 type steps, 2 weights, 3 shadows and one easing curve. That is the whole
design — a colour, radius, shadow or curve not on that list is one you
invented. An absence here is an instruction rather than a gap: fill nothing in
from convention.

**Scope.** A visual language, not a page: sections, copy, information
architecture and imagery are yours, and the silence on them is deliberate.

**Habits to suppress.** A model asked for a page returns the average of every
page, and the average is recognisable. None of this is here unless it was
measured.

- **Copy** — no small uppercase letterspaced line over the headline
  (`FOR DEVELOPERS`, `INTRODUCING`); no feature title built from two abstract
  nouns ("Seamless Integration"); no *seamlessly*, *effortlessly*, *unlock*,
  *elevate*, *empower*, *transform*, *leverage*, *delve* — density is the tell
  rather than any one word; no "Get started" band; no invented testimonial,
  customer logo, round statistic, or "Sarah Johnson, Head of Operations" over a
  generated avatar; no caption on a thing that already says what it is.
- **Structure** — not hero, logo wall, three feature cards, three-tier pricing
  with the middle plan lifted, FAQ accordion, closing CTA, four-column footer;
  not steps numbered 01 / 02 / 03; not three of anything by default; not every
  section the same width, centred, at the same padding; not a filled button
  beside an outlined one, arrow welded to the label.
- **Surface** — not Inter unless this file names it, and not a stock Tailwind
  palette (`indigo-600`, untouched `zinc` and `slate`); no
  indigo-to-violet-to-pink gradient; no gradient-filled heading; no
  `rounded-2xl shadow-lg p-6` on everything; no glass panel; no blurred glow
  behind the hero; no emoji standing in for an icon; no icon in a tinted rounded
  square; no `Sparkles`, `ArrowRight` or `Zap`; no fake browser or terminal
  chrome — traffic-light dots, a filename bar — around a code sample; no stock
  photograph of people at a laptop and no floating 3D blob.
- **Motion** — this file measures its own. Do not leave hover states doing
  nothing, do not snap where it declares an easing curve, and do not put one
  fade-in-up on every element on the page.

The tell under all of them is uniformity — one radius, one border, one shadow,
one gap everywhere, because nothing was decided.
